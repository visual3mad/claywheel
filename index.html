QUICK REFERENCE

Keyboard shortcuts

Tools

Throw1

Drag over the clay: move out/in to widen/narrow and up/down to pull/compress.

Diameter2

Drag horizontally outward/inward to widen/narrow the local cross-section.

Deform3

Drag vertically to pull or compress a local section of the vessel.

Height4

Drag vertically to stretch or compress the whole vessel.

Groove5

Press or drag to cut a narrow circular groove at each height.

Ridge6

Press or drag to build a rounded raised ring at each height.

Sharpen7

Drag across a rounded transition to concentrate it into one sharper edge.

Smooth8

Drag along the profile to blend the local shape with neighboring rings.

Controls

Pressure

Sets tool strength, including groove depth and ridge height.

Influence

Sets the local profile area and groove/ridge width. Height affects the whole vessel.

Height

Set total height with the slider or the Exact height field.

Height lock

When on, Throw and Deform vertical pulls keep total height fixed. Height still changes it.

Preserve clay

For Diameter, compensates nearby radii to keep clay volume approximately constant.

Speed

The slider sets turntable speed in RPM (0–140).

Pause / resume wheel

Use the wheel button or press Space to toggle rotation.

Navigation & shortcuts

Orbit

Drag empty space in Model mode or drag anywhere in Observe mode.

Zoom

Scroll with a mouse or pinch with two fingers on touchscreens.
